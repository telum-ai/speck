# Epic evidence rollup

1. Require every story `validation-report.md` at `IMPL-GREEN` or higher, the
   epic `audit-report.md`, and the project evidence contract. Any story
   `NO-SHIP`, missing audit, or audit P0 stops validation.
2. Compute the maximum claim as
   `MIN(story states, MATRIX_GRAIN_CAP, GRAPH_CAP)`.
3. Cross-examine each cited artifact against the exact build SHA. Story labels
   do not prove the composed epic path.
4. Judge the axes independently: CORRECT from rollup/audit/matrix/graph/mutation;
   ON-CONTRACT from evidence-contract gates; FELT-GOOD and TASTE from distinct
   prior hostile-user judgments for consumer UX-RC+ only. A graph can prove
   traceability, completeness, and freshness, never quality.
5. For UI epics, require prior epic-level LARP evidence for the whole JTBD and
   prior visual evidence selected by the active recipe. Missing evidence routes
   back to its producer; validation does not produce it.
6. Device-walk criteria without the required human attestation cap the epic at
   `UX-RC` and block `SHIP-RC+`.
7. For backend/infra epics, adjudicate the recorded system-operational
   walkthrough and use the `API-RC` branch.
8. Every deferral names its reproduced attempt and readiness cap. An
   `autonomous-not-done` cold-start LARP is not a valid deferral.
