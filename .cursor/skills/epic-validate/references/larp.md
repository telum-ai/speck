# Epic LARP evidence adjudication

LARP is produced before epic validation. This node cross-examines it; it does
not run `speck-larp` or create a walkthrough.

- Require checked-in epic-level recordings for the exact build SHA and each
  primary persona/JTBD. Story recordings alone do not prove composition.
- Verify cold start, real navigation/auth, the complete job, failure recovery,
  first-time comprehension, and naive-hostile DOES-IT-WORK + IS-IT-GOOD.
- Confirm FELT-GOOD and TASTE have distinct judgments; accessibility output or
  screenshots cannot substitute for either.
- A dead end on the primary path caps at `IMPL-GREEN`. Missing, stale, or
  unreachable evidence routes back to `speck-larp`; an infrastructure blocker
  supports a cap only when its reproduced attempt is recorded.
- A high-impact product decision must cite its prior
  `speck-premise-challenge` verdict.

Graph evidence may prove traceability, completeness, and freshness. It cannot
raise a LARP quality verdict.
