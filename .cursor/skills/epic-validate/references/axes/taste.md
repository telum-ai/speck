# TASTE adjudication

For consumer `UX-RC+`, require prior connoisseur-hostile evidence over the
composed epic. Inspect the cited pixels and interaction recording. Judge
coherence, hierarchy, restraint, craft, product personality, and whether the
experience deserves the promise it makes.

Record GOOD or BAD with concrete evidence. Missing, surrogate, or story-only
evidence cannot establish epic TASTE and caps below `UX-RC`.
