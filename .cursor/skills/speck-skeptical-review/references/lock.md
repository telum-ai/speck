# speck-skeptical-review / lock

1. State the exact decision and the product/evidence constraints it must satisfy.
2. Require at least three materially different, plausible, in-scope alternatives. A renamed copy does not count. If only two exist, widen the mechanism or boundary.
3. Score 3–5 project-specific dimensions: paid-promise fit, trust, risk, reversibility, operating cost, speed, and substitute defensibility where value/price is involved.
4. For price/paywall decisions, name why the offer survives free general-purpose AI plus buyer effort; convenience alone is not a durable wedge.
5. Surface the full comparison before locking. Pick one option; “all equal” is not a decision.
6. Record:
   - Question and alternatives
   - Per-dimension tradeoffs
   - Locked choice and rationale tied to contract evidence
   - Consequences accepted
   - Revisit trigger
7. Append through `/speck-decision-log`; never silently replace an earlier lock. A supersession cites the prior DEC.
8. Return the lock to the calling skill.

Run for paid promise, differentiator, magic moment placement, architecture/stack, proof source, gate criteria, price, readiness target, and PROMISE/CONSTRAINT conflict. Skip only trivial implementation details such as a local variable name.
