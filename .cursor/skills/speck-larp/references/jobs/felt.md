1. Read `.speck/templates/story/persona-larp-template.md` and the product's intended emotional outcome.
2. Inspect every captured image itself, not only the AX tree. Ask what a discerning target user would notice, misunderstand, distrust, or wince at first.
3. Record at least two pixel-anchored observations per screen. Check duplication, clipping, overlap, off-screen actions, hierarchy, spacing, type/accent proliferation, icon/copy fit, placeholders, and emotional-tone match.
4. Exercise hesitation and recovery; judge orientation, confidence, effort, and whether the magic moment actually feels like the promised outcome.
5. A captured but unadjudicated screen is surrogate proof. Return a separate FELT-GOOD verdict with cited findings; any material confusion/disorientation blocks the axis.
6. `felt_axis: ai-verified` is valid on a clean hostile pass; optional human review may strengthen it but is not mandatory.
